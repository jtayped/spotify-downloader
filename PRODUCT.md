# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A small circle of friends and family downloading their own Spotify playlists and tracks as MP3s. Context: personal music archiving, offline listening, sharing a collection. They know what they want and expect the tool to get out of the way and just work.

## Product Purpose

Paste a Spotify URL, get a ZIP of MP3s. The app handles the Spotify metadata fetch, YouTube matching, download, and packaging. Success is a fast, frictionless download with no confusion about what's happening or where to click next.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, dependable, quiet confidence. Three words: precise, clean, trustworthy.

Emotional goal: the interface should feel like something a developer built for themselves and polished until it looked intentional. No wink, no gimmick — just a tool that clearly knows what it does.

## Anti-references

- **Sketchy downloader sites**: ad-heavy, cramped, warning-banner-heavy layouts that make users feel like they're doing something wrong.
- **SaaS marketing templates**: gradient heroes, "streamline your workflow", social proof counters, or any copy that sells instead of helps.
- **Overly playful / bubbly**: rounded everything, pastel palettes, casual-consumer softness that undersells the technical precision of the tool.
- **Bland gray default**: boilerplate admin panel energy — framework defaults with no personality, no considered choices.

## Design Principles

1. **Invisible scaffolding** — The UI exists to move the user from URL to ZIP. Every element either advances that goal or is removed.
2. **Legitimacy through craft** — Visual quality is the only thing separating this from a sketchy download site. Precise spacing, considered color, and deliberate typography signal that this is intentional software.
3. **Music-aware, not music-cute** — Album art and metadata are displayed with care, but the interface is a tool, not a music player. Content earns visual weight; chrome does not.
4. **Both themes as first-class citizens** — System preference is respected, so neither dark nor light can be an afterthought. Every surface must look considered in both.
5. **Linear/Raycast reference axis** — Sharp, not soft. Dense but legible. Dark-capable without being gloomy. Hierarchy through contrast, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline. Reduced motion respected for all transitions and animations. Color is never the sole carrier of meaning (status states use icons + labels alongside color). Keyboard navigation throughout.
